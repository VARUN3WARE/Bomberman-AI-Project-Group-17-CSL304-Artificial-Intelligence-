Bot AI Deep Dive - README


This document provides a specialized look at the bot's AI, its decision-making process, and the potential for future enhancements.

=========================

Bot Decision Priority List
=========================


The bot's actions are governed by a priority list, which is evaluated on every "think" tick. The bot executes the first valid action it can from the top of this list.

--------------------
1. Highest Priority: Evade Danger
--------------------

- **Trigger**: The bot is currently on a tile that is in the blast radius of an active bomb, and the bomb is about to explode.

- **Action**: The bot immediately enters the `evade` state. It uses the `find_safe_path` function to locate the best possible escape route. This function is designed to be intelligent, preferring open areas over dead ends.
- **Note**: This check overrides all other states and intentions. A bot will abandon chasing a player or breaking a wall if it needs to save itself.


--------------------
2. Chase & Attack
--------------------
- **Trigger**: The player is within the bot's `vision` range, and the bot is in the `chase` state.
- **Action**:
    - **Pathfinding**: If the bot doesn't have a path to the player, it will use the A* algorithm to generate one.
    - **Attack**: If the bot is close to the player (within 2 tiles), it will consider placing a bomb. The decision is based on its `aggression` setting.
    - **Safety First**: A bomb is only placed if the `bot_can_escape_after_placing` check confirms that the bot can get to safety before its own bomb detonates.

--------------------
3. Search & Destroy Walls
--------------------

- **Trigger**: The player is not in sight, and the bot is in the `search` state.
- **Action**:
    - **Targeting**: The bot identifies the nearest `SOFT` wall tile.
    - **Pathfinding**: It generates a path to a tile adjacent to the target wall.
    - **Bombing**: Once it reaches the wall, it may place a bomb to destroy it, again subject to the safety check.

--------------------
4. Follow Existing Path
--------------------
- **Trigger**: The bot has an active path from a previous decision (e.g., it's chasing the player or moving to a wall).
- **Action**: The bot takes the next step along its current path.
- **Path Invalidation**: If the next step on the path becomes blocked or dangerous, the bot will clear its path and make a new decision on the next tick.

--------------------
5. Lowest Priority: Random Move
--------------------
- **Trigger**: The bot has no other valid actions. This can happen if it's boxed in, its path is blocked, or it can't find a target.
- **Action**: The bot will choose a random, valid, and safe adjacent tile to move to. This ensures the bot is always doing something and doesn't get stuck.

=========================
Future Scope & AI Improvements
=========================


The current AI is robust, but there are many exciting possibilities for making it even more intelligent and challenging.

--------------------
1. Enhanced Search and Map Control
--------------------
- **Smarter Targeting**: Instead of just targeting the nearest soft wall, the AI could prioritize walls that open up larger sections of the map or lead to areas it hasn't explored.
- **Choke Point Control**: The AI could learn to identify and control choke points on the map, placing bombs to trap players or cut off their escape routes.

--------------------
2. Predictive Targeting
--------------------
- The current AI chases the player's last known position. A more advanced version could analyze the player's movement patterns to predict where they are going and move to intercept them.

--------------------
3. Team-Based AI
--------------------
- **Coordinated Attacks**: If multiple bots are in play, they could communicate or be aware of each other's state. They could work together to corner a player from multiple directions.
- **Friendly Fire Avoidance**: Bots could be programmed to not place bombs if the blast would trap or kill a fellow bot.

--------------------
4. Power-Up Awareness (If Added)
--------------------
- If power-ups (e.g., more bombs, increased speed) were added to the game, the AI would need to be updated to:
    - Recognize and prioritize collecting valuable power-ups.
    - Be aware of the player's power-ups (e.g., be more cautious if the player has a larger bomb radius).

--------------------
5. Baiting and Trapping
--------------------
- A truly advanced AI could engage in psychological warfare. For example, it could place a bomb to force a player to move in a certain direction, while it positions itself for a follow-up attack, effectively baiting the player into a trap.

--------------------
6. Machine Learning
--------------------
- **Reinforcement Learning**: The ultimate step would be to build a model using reinforcement learning. The AI would play the game thousands of times, learning from its mistakes and successes.
- **Outcome**: This could lead to emergent strategies and behaviors that a human programmer might not even consider, resulting in an exceptionally challenging and human-like opponent.
